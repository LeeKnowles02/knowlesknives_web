Our Story

Knowles Knives was born from a lifelong fascination with blades — their history, their utility, and the quiet artistry of a well-made knife. What began as weekend forging in a small workshop has grown into a dedicated craft, producing knives that are as practical as they are beautiful.

Every knife that leaves our workshop is individually hand-finished. We work with premium steels, carefully selected handle materials, and time-honoured techniques refined through years of practice. Whether you are a chef seeking the perfect kitchen companion, a hunter who demands reliability in the field, a collector looking for a statement piece, or someone searching for a meaningful gift — we craft blades with purpose.

We believe a great knife should be used, not just displayed. Our custom commissions allow you to be part of the creative process, from initial design sketches through to the final edge. No two Knowles knives are exactly alike — and that is precisely the point.
